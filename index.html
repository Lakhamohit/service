The Pipeline

What I can handle.

Each stage can be commissioned independently, or combined into a complete production workflow.

Development 01

Concept Development

Story breakdowns, character design sheets, visual references, and page thumbnails that establish the direction before final artwork begins.

Editorial 02

Pre-Production Editing

Script and layout review focused on pacing, panel flow, dialogue, visual clarity, and storytelling — helping prevent expensive revisions later.

Artwork 03

Line Art & Illustration

Full penciling and inking, panel by panel, adapted to the visual language of your project — from clean line work to expressive and textured finishes.

Colour 04

Coloring

Flats, rendering, atmosphere, and lighting designed to work with the line art while maintaining a consistent visual identity across the entire book.

Typography 05

Lettering

Dialogue balloons, captions, sound effects, and page typography carefully placed so the story reads naturally without competing with the artwork.

Final 06

Post-Production

Final continuity checks, lettering and color corrections, page cleanup, export preparation, and production-ready files for print or digital publishing.

Workflow

One process. Six stages.

01 / Concept Define the visual direction.
02 / Edit Lock story and page flow.
03 / Draw Build the finished artwork.
04 / Colour Add atmosphere and depth.
05 / Letter Make every page readable.
06 / Deliver Prepare final production files.
